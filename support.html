N x N account and data deletion

You can delete your N x N account in the Android app: My → Account → Delete Account. Reauthenticate with the same Google account and confirm. This deletes the N x N account, not your Google or Apple account.

Without the app, email leedi@pepper-factory.com from the address associated with your N x N sign-in. Use the subject “N x N account deletion”. State that you want to delete the account and profile data. Include share links if you also want shared packages removed. Never send a password, one-time code or identity document. If you want only a shared package deleted, say so; your account will remain.

We verify account ownership before deleting data and normally respond within 30 days. After verification we remove the uploaded profile photo and delete the Firebase identity, including its display name, email and provider association. Firebase may require up to 180 days to remove deleted authentication data from live and backup systems; its security IP logs remain for a few weeks.

Local boards and exported files remain on your device until you delete them or clear app storage. Recipients’ saved copies cannot be recalled. Ordinary shares are not linked to your account and expire through their seven-day lifecycle or import claim; include their links to request earlier removal. Unlinked analytics and aggregate statistics are handled under the privacy policy and are not automatically located by an account deletion request.

FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
How do I sync data across devices?
Our apps use iCloud to sync data automatically. Make sure iCloud is enabled for the app in your device settings (Settings > Apple ID > iCloud).
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select the subscription you want to cancel and follow the instructions.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please email us with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful!
