Privacy Policy

Pepper Factory Apps

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Pepper Factory ("we," "us," or "our") operates the following mobile applications (collectively, the "Services"): OneThing, Weekly, Workout Timy, Ad Revenue Tracker, Pop, MojiBoard, and N x N.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect varies depending on which app you use:

OneThing

  • Timer records (focus time, break time, session counts)
  • To-do lists and task content
  • D-Day events (dates and titles)
  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

Weekly

  • Habit data (names, icons, colors, target days)
  • Completion records and history
  • Reminder settings
  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

Workout Timy

  • Workout data (timer settings, sets, reps, rest intervals)
  • Exercise history and duration
  • Health data synced with Apple HealthKit (with your permission)
  • Subscription status

Ad Revenue Tracker

  • Google Account information (for authentication)
  • AdMob/AdSense revenue data (accessed via Google API)
  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

Pop

  • No personal information is collected
  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

MojiBoard

  • Favorites and recently copied emoticons (stored locally on device)
  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

N x N

  • Photos and videos you select from your library or capture with the camera (processed locally on device)
  • Audio recorded with the microphone for board videos (processed locally on device)
  • Exported board videos saved to your Photo Library (with your permission)
  • App usage statistics and pseudonymous app/device identifiers

N x N Android — account, sharing and analytics

Android supplement updated October 26, 2026. This section explains N x N Android 0.1.1 and takes precedence over general statements about local-only storage. Core board editing and export work without an account. Ads, subscriptions, cloud board backup and beta screenshot reporting are disabled in this Android release.

Photos, videos, board text and drawings are normally processed and stored on your device. They are sent off-device when you choose to upload a profile photo or create a board share. Shared board packages can include the selected media, text, drawings, layout and a preview. Anyone with the share link and, when set, its password can access the shared content. Password protection controls server access; it is not end-to-end encryption.

Optional Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Firebase processes the account identifier, provider information, display name, email address and provider profile image where supplied. A profile photo you choose to upload is stored on our Cloudflare infrastructure. These data support sign-in, profile management and account security. Your Google or Apple account password is not provided to N x N.

Firebase Analytics, Amplitude and our Cloudflare telemetry process app interactions, installation or device identifiers, app/OS versions, device model, language, and operational information such as download duration and failure codes. Analytics providers may derive approximate location from IP addresses. Our resource service receives search terms you enter to return matching templates or stickers. Network providers and Firebase process IP addresses and request information for security and abuse prevention. Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous, rather than a guarantee of anonymity.

Google/Firebase, Amplitude and Cloudflare process information to provide authentication, analytics, hosting and delivery services. Transfers use HTTPS. Choosing a share deliberately makes its selected content available to recipients. We do not sell your personal information. Permissions are used for the feature you invoke; granting photo access does not upload your entire library.

Account and profile information remains until you change it or delete the N x N account. The in-app deletion flow removes the uploaded profile image and requests deletion of the Firebase identity. Firebase states that authentication IP logs are retained for a few weeks and deleted authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days. Ordinary board shares are removed after their import claim or by a seven-day object lifecycle. Official persistent links remain until removed. Unlinked analytics and aggregate service statistics follow their separate provider or service retention settings; deleting an account does not automatically identify and erase those records.

You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting leedi@pepper-factory.com. For account deletion without the app, use the steps linked below. Include a share link if you also want its uploaded package removed, because ordinary shares are not tied to a sign-in account. Local boards, exported media and recipients’ copies are controlled separately. Our services are not directed to children under 13.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Services
  • To sync your data across devices (via iCloud where applicable)
  • To send notifications and reminders you've configured
  • To process subscriptions and payments
  • To improve our Services through anonymized analytics

3. Data Storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device and, where applicable, in your personal iCloud account. N x N Android account, profile, sharing and analytics data are handled as described in its supplement above.

  • Local data is stored using Apple's secure frameworks (SwiftData)
  • iCloud synchronization follows Apple's security policies
  • Authentication tokens (Ad Revenue Tracker) are stored in iOS Keychain
  • HealthKit data (Workout Timy) is managed by Apple's secure framework

4. Third-Party Services

Our apps integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud - Data synchronization
  • Apple HealthKit - Workout data recording
  • Google Sign-In & AdMob/AdSense API - Authentication and data access
  • Google AdMob - Advertisements (free versions)
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management
  • Amplitude - Anonymized analytics

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are a resident of the EEA or California, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to deletion: Delete your data by clearing app data or uninstalling
  • Right to data portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to withdraw consent: Revoke permissions at any time
  • Right to opt-out: We do not sell your personal information

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
